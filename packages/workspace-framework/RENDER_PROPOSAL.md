## 🎯 WHY ASTRO.JS AS FOUNDATION

**Technical Benefits** (Original Insight):

> "hanya di astro bahkan kita bisa memilih interaktifitasnya mau di render pakai apa, svelte, react, atau bahkan qwik sesuai dengan kebutuhan preloading nya masing-masing"

**Strategic Implementation**:

```astro
<!-- Per-component optimization -->
<TerminalWidget client:load />      <!-- Svelte: lightweight -->
<FileManager client:visible />      <!-- React: ecosystem -->
<SystemMonitor client:idle />       <!-- Qwik: resumability -->
<WindowManager />                   <!-- Vanilla: performance -->
```
