# Skill: Technical Article Perfectionist

Standard for refining technical content to be high-impact, authentic, and "AI-slop free" while maintaining maximum information density.

## 1. Writing Philosophy: "The Signal over Noise"

- **Kill the Fluff**: Delete generic introductions, transition words like "Furthermore" or "In conclusion," and vague summaries.
- **Opinions are Features**: Don't just report; analyze. Provide a clear technical or philosophical stance.
- **Show, Don't Just Tell**: Use code, diagrams, or specific data points (versions, dates, benchmarks) to back every claim.
- **Technical Density**: Maintain the depth. If a concept is complex (e.g., `Cow<str>`), explain it through a visual or analogy, never by "dumbing it down."

## 2. Structural Guidelines

### The Hook (Opening)

- Start with a problem, a contradiction, or a "behind the scenes" fact that isn't public knowledge.
- Avoid "In the digital era..." or "AI is growing fast...".

### The Meat (Content)

- Use **Hierarchical Headings** to group logical segments.
- Insert **Mermaid Diagrams** for every complex flow or comparison.
- Use **Decision Tables** to simplify complex logic or "if-this-then-that" scenarios (Tribal Knowledge).
- Define **Explicit Boundaries**: Clearly state what a technology or approach _cannot_ do to manage expectations.
- Use **GitHub Alerts** (NOTE, TIP, IMPORTANT) for critical side-information or warnings.
- Keep paragraphs short (max 4-5 lines) to maintain reading momentum.

### The Code (Technicality)

- Code snippets must be "copy-pasteable" or "mentally executable."
- Include comments in code that explain the _why_, not just the _what_.

## 3. Language & Tone (Sandikodev Voice)

- **Technical Indonesian**: Use natural developer slang where appropriate (e.g., "ngalam," "meleset," "jebakan").
- **Direct Address**: Use "Kamu" or "Kita" to create a sense of pair-programming/mentorship.
- **Metaphors**: Use physical-world metaphors for abstract concepts (e.g., "Jubah, Badan, Jantung" for OS layers).
- **Authentic Citations**: Always link to original sources (papers, PRs, official announcements) to build trust and authority.

## 4. Anti-AI Writing Patterns (Checklist)

- [ ] No "double-edged sword" or "game changer" clichés.
- [ ] No repetitive sentence starters ("Moreover," "Additionally").
- [ ] No vague "future is bright" conclusions. Replace with a "Call to Action" or a "Deep Question."
- [ ] No hallucinated facts. Every date and version must be verified against the workspace or external docs.

## 5. Perfectionist Workflow

1.  **Context Mapping**: Cross-reference the article with related posts in the blog.
2.  **Redundancy Audit**: Merge repetitive points.
3.  **Visualization Injection**: Add Mermaid diagrams where text feels too heavy.
4.  **Formatting Polish**: Ensure markdown tags (bolding, lists) are used to aid scannability.
5.  **Final Polish**: Read aloud to ensure the flow feels human and conversational.
